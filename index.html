THE LAST SPARK — COMBAT DOSSIER

In a digital universe called The Spectrum, millions of colorful energy particles once lived peacefully. But a corruption known as The Void has turned particles into aggressive, unstable swarm dots.

You control Spark-01, a tiny ball of living lightning. Survive the chaos, collect energy crystals, evolve your weapons, and destroy the Void Core Titans!

PLAYER 1 (SPARK-01 • CYAN) CONTROLS

  • WASD: Move Spark-01 with lightning inertia
  • Mouse Cursor / Left Click: Aim & Fire weapons (or press C for Auto-Fire)
  • [Q] / Right Click: Lightning Dash
  • [E]: Aegis Orbital Shield
  • [R]: Electric Shockwave
  • [SPACE]: Spectrum Overdrive
  • Gamepad 1: Full twin-stick controls supported!

PLAYER 2 (NOVA-02 • MAGENTA) CONTROLS (LOCAL CO-OP)

  • Arrow Keys / [I, J, K, L]: Move Nova-02
  • [O] / Right Ctrl / Numpad 0: Fire weapons (aims in move direction or nearest target)
  • [U] / Right Shift / Numpad 1: Lightning Dash
  • [ [ ] / Numpad 2: Aegis Shield
  • [ ] ] / Numpad 3: Electric Shockwave
  • [Enter] / Numpad Enter: Spectrum Overdrive
  • Gamepad 2: Plug in a 2nd controller for instant twin-stick co-op!

CO-OP REVIVAL & SYNERGY

  • ⚡ Energy Tether Revive: If a player falls to 0 HP, stand within their distress radius for 2.2s to revive them with 45% HP!
  • ✨ Harmonic Convergence: When both players trigger Overdrive simultaneously, a screen-clearing plasma laser connects them, vaporizing enemies between them!

HOSTILE ENTITY THREAT REGISTER

  • ● Chasers (Red): Rush straight toward you in swarms.
  • ● Speeders (Yellow): Extremely fast, erratic bursts.
  • ● Tanks (Purple): Massive armored orbs absorbing heavy fire.
  • ● Splitters (Green): Divide into mini-dots upon death.
  • ● Snipers (Blue): Telegraph laser lines and fire long-range bolts.
  • ● Exploders (Orange): Rapidly flash when close and explode with AoE blast!
  • ● The Void Titan (Boss): Corrupted core with rotating lasers and bullet rings.
